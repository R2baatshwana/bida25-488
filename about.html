Our Story

Understanding the roots of Gaborone's intellectual cafe.

Brewing Wisdom since 2026

Tlhalefo Cafe was founded with a straightforward, yet ambitious vision: to construct a hub for thinkers, students, and creators right in the heart of Gaborone.

We saw a growing need for a professional space that transcends a typical restaurant environment. Our cafe is designed to be your secondary office, your group study room, and your quiet corner for reflection. We believe that great ideas are born over great, relaxing coffee.

We take immense pride in supporting local farmers across Botswana. Every single bean we brew is ethically and locally sourced, ensuring not only an unmatched depth of flavor but also a thriving local economy. Welcome to Tlhalefo.
